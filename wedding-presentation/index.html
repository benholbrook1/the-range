The brief

What we’re planning for

Where you live

One of you in Waterloo, one in Burlington — so each idea splits the difference or leans toward a home base.

The musts

Food included in the package. Room for ~130 people. Atmosphere over formality — guests should feel the vibe.

The budget

$40,000 all-in for venue + food + bar + photo + music + florals + the extras that make it feel like you.

Idea 01 · Burlington

Lakeside Glow

All-inclusive dinner packages on the water — easy logistics, sunset photos, and a room that already feels like a celebration.

Marquis Gardens · Burlington

Why this works

Built for package weddings: hors d’oeuvres, multi-course dinner, late-night table, and beverage service — so food is locked in from day one.

The vibe

  • Blue Water Ballroom — polished, bright, ready to party
  • Optional waterfront patio ceremony
  • ~20–35 min from Burlington · ~70–85 min from Waterloo
  • Capacity well above 130 — room to dance

What’s in the package

  • Ruby package from ~$105/person + HST
  • Passed hors d’oeuvres + 3-course dinner
  • Premium beverage service + wine at dinner
  • Late-night table included

Idea 02 · Midway

Stone & Stream

Ancaster Mill — waterfall views, timber beams, and farm-to-table dining halfway between both of you.

Ancaster Mill · Ancaster

Why this works

The romance is already in the architecture. Inclusive Pearle-style packages cover space, tables, staff, coordinator, and seasonal menus — you show up for the vibes.

The vibe

  • Stone mill beside a waterfall + streamside chapel
  • Governor’s Room seats ~130 — exact fit
  • ~20 min from Burlington · ~55–70 min from Waterloo
  • Feels special without trying too hard

What’s in the package

  • Event space, harvest tables, china & staff
  • Lead event coordinator on the day
  • Farm-to-table / seasonal dinner menus
  • Ceremony option at Streamside Chapel

Idea 03 · Waterloo

Fairway Golden Hour

Grey Silo Golf Club — patio overlooking the course, Timber Room for 130, and food that guests actually rave about.

Grey Silo Golf Club · Waterloo

Why this works

Scenic without stiff. On-site catering (plated, buffet, stations), flexible bar options, and a wraparound deck that keeps the party outdoors until the stars show up.

The vibe

  • Timber Room — oak bar, beams, max 130
  • Patio over the Grand River / Rim Park views
  • ~15–25 min from Waterloo · ~70–90 min from Burlington
  • Relaxed “best wedding I’ve been to” energy

What’s in the package

  • Custom menus via on-site F&B team
  • Buffet or plated + cocktail hour options
  • Late-night stations (yes, even poutine)
  • Room access the night before to decorate

  1. Book venue tastings Marquis Gardens + Ancaster Mill first — clearest food packages. Add Grey Silo if you want the Waterloo patio night.
